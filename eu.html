Voice of Pages

Privacy Policy for the EEA, the UK and Switzerland
Effective: November 12, 2026

This version applies in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland (GDPR, UK GDPR, Swiss FADP). Other countries: general policy.

1. Controller

The controller is Duendest, the developer of Voice of Pages. For any question about your data and to exercise your rights: duendest@duendest.com.

2. What stays on your device

The files, texts, books and photos you open, your library, bookmarks and reading progress are stored only on your device; camera photos are not sent anywhere except for text recognition (see the table below); offline, text recognition (Google ML Kit, Tesseract) runs on the device. If you turn on the Voice of Pages accessibility service, on-screen text from other apps is used only to read it aloud at that moment and is neither stored nor sent. We don't collect any of this.

4. Recipients

Google Ireland Ltd. and Google LLC (AdMob, Firebase, Google Cloud Vision, Google Play, Google Sign-In, Google Drive), as processors or independent controllers under their terms: Google Privacy Policy, how Google uses data from partner apps. Book catalogs (Open Library, Internet Archive, Wikisource, OPDS) and the sites you open receive your IP address when contacted. We don't sell your data.

5. Transfers outside the EEA

Google may process data in the United States and other countries. Transfers rely on the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework, in which Google LLC participates, and on the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

6. Retention

Photos sent for recognition are not stored by us. Daily usage counters are deleted after 30 days. Crashlytics crash reports are kept for up to 90 days. User-level Analytics data for no longer than 14 months. Ad data is kept by Google under its own rules. Data on the device is kept until you delete it in the app, clear the app's data or uninstall it.

7. Your rights

  • access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability;
  • objection to processing based on legitimate interest;
  • withdrawing consent to ads and usage statistics at any time: app → Profile → Subscription → “Ad privacy settings”. Withdrawal does not affect processing before it;
  • resetting the advertising ID: Android settings → Privacy → Ads;
  • lodging a complaint with the data protection authority where you live or work.

To exercise your rights, write to duendest@duendest.com. We reply within one month.

8. Children

The app is not directed at children under 16, and we don't knowingly process their data.

9. Changes

A new version is published on this page with a new date. We will tell you about material changes in the app.
